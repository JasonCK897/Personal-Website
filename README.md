# Jason Christov Kesuma - Portfolio & Engineering Showcase

A modern, high-performance Single Page Application (SPA) portfolio website for **Jason Christov Kesuma**, an undergraduate Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Ma Chung specializing in **Full-Stack Web Engineering**, **Embedded Systems**, **Machine Learning & HMI Research**, and **Web3**.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [React 18](https://react.dev/) + [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk & Inter via Google Fonts
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/) & [React Icons](https://react-icons.github.io/react-icons/)
- **Forms**: Direct Formspree API with mailto fallback

---

## Architecture & Visual Design Highlights

1. **Warm Cream Canvas & Contrasting Section Boxes**:
   - Architectural warm cream background (`#F5F2EB`) providing a relaxed, high-end base.
   - Every single section sits inside an elevated, contrasting card box (`#FFFFFF` with refined borders, deep navy accents, and soft ambient shadows).
2. **Interactive Motion & Polish**:
   - Interactive mouse-following particle grid canvas in the Hero section.
   - Double trail-orbit animation with electric sapphire & sky blue trails.
   - Modern geometric monogram badge (`JCK`).
   - Interactive domain explorer pills and hover lift effects.
3. **Multi-Disciplinary Scope**:
   - Balanced representation across **Web Engineering**, **Embedded Systems**, **Machine Learning & HMI**, and **Web3**.
   - Dedicated **Research & Publications** section documenting academic manuscripts and ongoing HMI laboratory studies at Universitas Ma Chung.
4. **Direct Channel Cards**:
   - Clean, direct-link cards for Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and Instagram without exposing messy raw URLs.
